What is TPC?

Making it easy for people to care

The People's Community is a set of simple, accessible tools that help individuals and small groups share skills, exchange resources, coordinate care, and build stronger, more resilient lives — together.

No organization. No hierarchy. No gatekeeping. Just people showing up for each other, with the right tools to make it work.

It starts with showing up

Whether you want to care for the people around you or help build the tools that make it possible — there's a place for you here.
